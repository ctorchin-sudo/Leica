# Blue Shield of California — Opioid Prescribing Guidelines Research

## Overview

Blue Shield of California (the BCBS affiliate in California) has been a leader in opioid prescribing reform through its **Narcotic Safety Initiative (NSI)**, launched in 2015. The plan serves over 4 million members.

---

## Narcotic Safety Initiative (NSI)

### Goals & Timeline
- Launched in **2015** with the goal of cutting opioid use in half by 2018.
- First wave of interventions began **July 2015**, targeting extended-release (ER) narcotics by implementing a prior authorization requirement for ER oxycodone (OxyContin).

### Outcomes (as of 2019)
- **56% reduction** in overall opioid use among members with chronic, non-cancer pain (compared to 2014 baseline).
- **33% decrease** in average daily dose among chronic users.
- **60%+ reduction** in members on the highest doses of opioids.
- **24% reduction** in members taking opioid + benzodiazepine combinations.
- **36% drop** in monthly rates of ER opioid initiation (among 880,000 enrollees studied).

### Key Intervention Components
1. **Prescriber education** — Programs promoting prudent prescribing and proactive opioid management.
2. **Member programs** — Access to pain management, addiction, and substance abuse programs.
3. **Formulary access to MAT** — Improved formulary access to medication-assisted treatments for substance use disorders.
4. **Fraud, waste & abuse detection** — Enhanced monitoring and management.
5. **CURES database participation** — Collaboration with the California Department of Justice to educate providers on California's prescription drug monitoring program.

## Prior Authorization Requirements for Opioids

### General PA Criteria
- Prior authorization is required for **extended-release opioids** (e.g., OxyContin, Opana ER, Oxaydo).
- Prescribing physician must submit:
  - Patient's diagnosis
  - Previous treatments for pain (non-drug treatments, non-opioid medications, and prior opioid medications)
  - Medical necessity justification for opioid vs. non-opioid therapy
  - A treatment plan for pain management

### Clinical Criteria for Opioid PA (from formulary policy documents)
- Pain must be **severe enough to require opioid treatment**.
- Must **not be used with other short-acting narcotics** (for ER formulations).
- A **documented patient-specific treatment plan** is required, including:
  - Baseline pain intensity score
  - Functional interference score
  - Potential misuse assessment
  - Monitoring plan for adverse side effects
  - Plan to taper down opioid use
- Dose must **not exceed FDA maximum**.
- Total dosage must be consolidated to the **least number of higher-strength dosage forms**.

### Duration-Based Requirements
- If opioid use is expected to exceed **60 days**, the prescribing or consulting doctor must be in **pain management**.
- Pain must not be removable or otherwise treatable by other modalities (e.g., acupuncture, massage therapy, physical therapy).

### MME (Morphine Milligram Equivalent) Threshold
- For total daily narcotic use **above 90 MME**, additional clinical review applies.
- Blue Shield emphasizes using the **lowest dose possible** when opioids are needed and having a taper plan.

## Medicare Part D — Opioid-Specific PA Questions

Medicare Part D prior authorization forms include opioid-specific questions:
- "Are you aware of other opioid prescribers for this enrollee?"
- "Is the stated daily MED dose noted medically necessary?"
- "Would a lower total daily MED dose be insufficient to control the enrollee's pain?"

## Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) Coverage

- Blue Shield covers **buprenorphine, methadone, and naltrexone** for opioid use disorder.
- **No prior authorization required** for FDA-approved opioid use disorder medications (per California's 2022 law).
- Members receive immediate access through in-network providers.

### Buprenorphine Extended-Release (Sublocade) — Specific Criteria
- Requires clinical review and authorization prior to administration.
- Patient must have been initiated on **8–24 mg of transmucosal buprenorphine** for at least **7 days** before injection.

---

## Naloxone Coverage

- **No cost-sharing** for Blue Shield members.
- Available through network pharmacies and distribution programs.
- Compliance with both federal mental health parity laws and California's state regulations.

---

## Pharmacy Claims Processing

- Effective **January 1, 2025**, Blue Shield of California pharmacy claims are processed by **Abarca Health** (previously managed differently).
- The Pharmacy and Therapeutics (P&T) Committee considers safety, efficacy, FDA bioequivalency data, and cost when selecting formulary medications.

---

## Electronic Prior Authorization (ePA)

- Providers using **Surescripts** or **CoverMyMeds** EHR platforms can submit ePA requests.
- Provides real-time response at point of care.
- Can identify drugs that don't require prior authorization.

## California State Context

- California law (2022) prohibits prior authorization for FDA-approved opioid use disorder medications.
- The CURES (Controlled Substance Utilization Review and Evaluation System) database is California's prescription drug monitoring program; Blue Shield participates in educating providers about it.
- In 2023, approximately **8,000 Californians** died from overdoses (state record high).
- Approximately **5.36 million** Californians aged 12+ have a substance use disorder, with 90% not receiving specialty treatment.
